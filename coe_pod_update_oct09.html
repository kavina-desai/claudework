Bet 1: Matchmaking (Intelligent Retrieval & Ranking)
ETA Mon, 12 Oct
 Ongoing
Development in progress; v1 goes live Mon 12 Oct at 40% of Mumbai new DAU
Goal: Fill every jobseeker's feed with jobs they'll actually act on. Today, more than half of applies fall outside the stated role category, and hand-maintained related-category lists were never checked against behaviour.
Approach:
  • Retrieval: a related-category config built from 4 months of Mumbai behaviour decides which categories enter the feed; categories proven not to convert are removed
  • Ranking: a separate scorecard for each role category orders jobs by distance, freshness, salary/experience fit, posting quality and skill match
  • PRD
Bet 2: Scalable Data Collection
Dev start Mon, 12 Oct
 Ongoing
Experiment design and graph closed; in exploration/HLD phase, dev to start Mon 12 Oct
Goal: Collect richer candidate data with fewer questions, and improve matching.
Approach:
  • A knowledge graph built from connected calls suggests 6 job titles per candidate: 4 from the graph, plus 2 matched to their background
  • Ask only the questions employers in those roles actually check, weighted by job-form fill rates
  • PVD
